Connecting your WhatsApp number to the assistant

This links your existing WhatsApp Business number to __PROJECT__ so the assistant can reply to customers automatically. You keep using WhatsApp Business on your phone exactly as today. Both stay live on the same number and messages stay in sync.

Open this page on a computer, with your phone next to you. Meta will show a QR code on this screen that you scan with the WhatsApp Business app, the same way WhatsApp Web works, so it cannot be the phone showing the code and scanning it.

Before you connect anything, you should know exactly what changes. Some of it is permanent. Most of it is small. One item costs money that you do not pay today. We would rather you refuse now than discover something in week three.

Everything below comes from Meta's own documentation, and the sources are at the bottom of this page. You have to accept these points before the connect button becomes available.

1. Your chats are not deleted

Your existing conversations stay on your phone. Meta's documentation is explicit: with Coexistence "you will be able to use both the WhatsApp Business app and the partner's app concurrently, and your messaging history will be preserved."

There is a real reason for the worry, though. One way of connecting a number does destroy the history: deleting the number from the WhatsApp Business app and registering it fresh on the Cloud API. Meta warns that in that case "your existing messaging history will be lost." That is the fast method, and avoiding it is why this setup has taken as long as it has.

Back up before you scan anyway. Google Drive on Android, iCloud on iPhone, five minutes. A backup turns any surprise into an inconvenience instead of a loss.

2. What you lose permanently

WhatsApp for Windows stops working with this number. After connecting, your linked devices are signed out once and you re-link them. WhatsApp Web comes back. The Windows desktop app does not: Meta states that "WhatsApp for Windows and WhatsApp for WearOS remain unsupported after relinking."

If anyone on your team answers customers from the Windows desktop app, they will need to use WhatsApp Web in a browser instead. Please check this before you decide. Everything else on this list can be undone by re-linking. This one cannot.

3. Features that get switched off

For one-to-one chats, Meta disables:

  • Disappearing messages
  • View once
  • Live location
  • Broadcast lists, including the ability to create new ones

Groups keep working on your phone, but they are not synchronised with the assistant, which will not see or reply in them.

The broadcast one matters most. If you currently send promotions or seasonal offers to past customers through broadcast lists, that route closes. The replacement is Meta's approved marketing templates, which reach more people but cost money per message (see section 5) and need Meta's approval for each template you write. If broadcasts are part of how you sell today, tell us and we will plan the replacement before you connect.

4. One-time disruption, and one ongoing rule

Once, at connection time:

  • All companion devices are signed out and need re-linking.
  • You have 24 hours from scanning to finish syncing your history. Miss that window and the whole process has to be undone and repeated.

Ongoing, for as long as you use this:

  • The WhatsApp Business app must be opened on the phone at least once every 14 days. If it is not, Meta drops the connection and the assistant stops working until someone reconnects it.
  • The app must be version 2.24.17 or newer.

Treat that 14-day rule as an operational risk worth planning for. If the phone holding this number goes on a long trip or sits unused during low season, the assistant goes quiet. Decide now whose phone this lives on and who is responsible for opening it.

5. What it costs

Today the WhatsApp Business app is free. The platform the assistant runs on is not, and Meta requires a payment card on your WhatsApp Business Account before it will connect at all.

Since 1 July 2025, Meta charges per message, and only for template messages that are delivered:

The cost appears when you want to re-open a conversation that has gone cold, which is the same follow-up behaviour that recovers rentals you would otherwise lose.

We cannot predict your monthly bill, because it depends on how many cold leads you decide to chase. Meta removed the free monthly allowance it used to give in July 2025, so charging starts from the first paid message.

6. Your customer data

Up to 6 months of your existing chat history synchronises from the phone into the platform when you connect. After that, messages to and from this number flow through the system we operate for you.

Conversations you have already had with customers become visible in the CRM we built for you, including names, phone numbers and anything customers sent you. We hold that data to run your assistant and for nothing else. We do not sell it and we do not use it for other clients.

Two obligations follow, and they sit with you as the business:

  • Your privacy policy needs to say that an automated system processes customer messages. The policy Meta required is published at sumba.balibestmotorcycle.com/bbm-privacy-policy and covers this. Your main site still needs its own copy.
  • Indonesian data protection law (UU PDP) applies to you as the data controller. We act as the processor. If you want that relationship documented formally, ask and we will prepare it.

If you would prefer the assistant not to receive the 6 months of history, say so before you scan. We can skip it, and the assistant will start with no memory of returning customers.

7. How the assistant and your team share one number

Both work on the same number at the same time. When someone on your team types a reply from the phone, the assistant detects it and stops replying to that customer automatically, so nobody gets two answers to one question. It stays quiet on that conversation until you release it.

There is also a master switch. You can turn the assistant off completely, for everyone, from the control panel. While it is off it still records every incoming lead and marks it as needing a reply, so nothing gets lost.

8. Reversing the decision

Meta supports offboarding, and if you later re-register the app on a new phone you are offered a pre-ticked option to reconnect everything.

What we cannot promise is that undoing it restores your setup to exactly how it is today. The Windows desktop app is the known casualty. In practice you can reverse this, but not for free.

9. Risks nobody controls

  • Message quality rating. If customers block or report the number, Meta lowers its rating and can limit how many people you may message. Automated follow-ups increase that exposure, which is why we keep them conservative.
  • Approval timing. Meta has to accept the connection. We believe your setup qualifies, but Meta decides, and a review can take weeks if they ask for one.
  • Policy changes. Meta changed its pricing model in July 2025 and its onboarding method in 2026. Both can change again.
