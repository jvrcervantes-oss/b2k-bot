Connect your WhatsApp Business number

This links your existing WhatsApp Business number to __PROJECT__ so the assistant can reply to customers automatically. You keep using WhatsApp Business on your phone exactly as today — both stay live on the same number, and messages stay in sync.

Before you tap the button — these are Meta's requirements, not ours, and the flow cannot be finished without them.
  • A payment card on your WhatsApp Business Account. Meta bills conversations to the account, so it will not connect without one.
  • Your linked devices get signed out once, when you connect. WhatsApp Web and any tablets need to be linked again afterwards.
  • You have 24 hours after scanning to finish syncing your chat history. Past that, the whole process has to be repeated from scratch.
  • WhatsApp Business app 2.24.17 or newer, and open it at least once every 14 days — Meta drops the link otherwise.
  • On one-to-one chats, broadcast lists, disappearing messages, view-once and live location are turned off. Groups are not synced.

What happens next

Meta opens a window where you pick your business, then shows a QR code. Scan it from WhatsApp Business → Settings → Linked devices. When it finishes, this page confirms it and we take over from here — you do not need to send us anything.
